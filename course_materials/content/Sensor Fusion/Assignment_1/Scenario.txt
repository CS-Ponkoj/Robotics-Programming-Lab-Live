An autonomous robot is navigating a complex environment. It uses a variety of sensors including GPS, Lidar, a camera, wheel encoders, and an accelerometer for localization and navigation purposes. The robot's task is to navigate from a known starting point to a designated target location, avoiding obstacles and optimizing its path based on sensor inputs and odometry data.

Challenge:
The environment in which the robot is operating is a 100m x 100m square area with multiple obstacles. The robot's starting coordinates are at (10, 10) and the target location is at (90, 90).

The robot is equipped with:
GPS: Provides an absolute position with an accuracy of ±3 meters.
Lidar: Detects obstacles up to 20 meters away with an accuracy of ±0.5 meters.
Camera: Identifies visual landmarks and can calculate distances to them with an accuracy of ±1 meter.
Wheel Encoders: Provide relative movement data, each encoder tick corresponds to a linear movement of 1 cm.
Accelerometer: Measures the acceleration in m/s² with a precision of ±0.1 m/s².

Tasks:
1. Path Planning with Sensor Fusion:

	Calculate the optimal path the robot should take to reach the target while avoiding obstacles detected by Lidar.
	Incorporate the inaccuracies of each sensor in your calculations.
	Assume a simple straight-line path is not viable due to the obstacles.

2. Localization Error Analysis:

	Calculate the cumulative localization error over the course of the journey considering the inaccuracies of GPS, Lidar, and Camera.
	Assume the robot checks its absolute position with GPS every 10 meters.

3. Odometry and Drift Calculation:

	Given that the robot has a drift of 0.5% in its wheel encoders, calculate the total drift error by the time it reaches the target.
	Assume the robot uses a differential drive mechanism and moves at an average speed of 1 m/s.

4. Acceleration Impact:

	If the robot has to stop suddenly due to an obstacle, calculate the minimum stopping distance given its current speed and accelerometer data.


Deliverables:
Provide detailed calculations and formulas used for each part of the task.
Include diagrams or graphs if necessary to illustrate the path and errors.
Discuss how sensor fusion helps in reducing overall navigation error and improving the robot's performance in this scenario.
